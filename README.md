
# Hospital Readmission Analysis

### Finding:

Encounters involving patients with three or more previous inpatient visits had a higher observed 30-day readmission rate than encounters involving patients with no previous inpatient visits.

### Evidence:
•	Encounter count: 101,766
•	Readmission count: 11,357
•	Readmission rate: 11.16%

### Business interpretation:
Previous inpatient utilization may help hospitals identify patients requiring stronger discharge planning and follow-up.

### Recommendation:
Consider prioritizing patients with repeated inpatient or emergency utilization for post-discharge follow-up, medication review and appointment coordination.
